## 0.2.0 — Studio overhaul

E8 Studio has grown from a focused E8 visualization into a cross-platform mathematical visualization studio while keeping the E8 root system at its center.

### Highlights

- Redesigned the desktop and mobile control systems around clear **View**, **Visuals**, and **Learn** workspaces, with persistent tabs and easier access to quality, motion, effects, and capture controls.
- Expanded the Studio to eight manually selectable views: Bloom, Platonic solids, E8 Coxeter, the 600-cell, regular 4D polytopes, E8 SDF, a generated rank-2 Root Lab, and E₆/E₇/E₈ Dynkin diagrams.
- Brought desktop and mobile features into closer alignment with broader model, palette, background, effect, camera, animation, and learning controls while retaining mobile performance safeguards.
- Improved rendering across E8 chords and roots, Platonic and 4D geometry, vertex nodes, backgrounds, effects, and the raymarched SDF view.
- Reworked selection and reset behavior so controls remain tied to the active model, incompatible settings stay out of the way, and panels reopen where the user left them.
- Expanded the educational experience with four learning paths, ten structured lessons, 30 guided Studio experiment steps, contextual explanations, mathematical references, quizzes, a glossary, a generated Root Lab, and a dedicated Dynkin reference view.
- Added persistent experiment progress, prerequisite guidance, cross-topic connections, and compact desktop/mobile coaching that keeps the visualization visible while learners investigate it.
- Strengthened standalone, web, desktop, and mobile build verification to keep the different distributions consistent.

Root Lab and Dynkin diagrams remain available for intentional mathematical exploration but are excluded from **Auto Model**, which stays focused on the Studio’s denser visual scenes.
